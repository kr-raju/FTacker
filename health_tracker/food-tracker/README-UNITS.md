# Food Tracker Unit Auto-Detection

The Food Tracker app includes an intelligent unit auto-detection feature that automatically suggests appropriate portion units based on the food item you enter. This makes meal tracking faster and more intuitive.

## How It Works

When you type a food name in the meal entry form, the app analyzes the text and automatically sets the most appropriate unit for that food. For example, if you type "coffee", it will suggest "cup(s)" as the unit, while if you type "chicken", it will suggest "oz" as the unit.

## Supported Food Categories and Units

### Liquids
- **Cup(s)**: coffee, tea, chai
- **Bowl**: soup, broth, stew
- **ml**: water, juice, milk, soda, smoothie, drink, latte, espresso, cappuccino

### Grains and Pasta
- **Cup(s)**: rice, pasta, cereal, oatmeal, quinoa, noodle, spaghetti, macaroni, biryani, pulao, risotto

### Bread and Baked Goods
- **Slice(s)**: cake, pie
- **Piece(s)**: bread, toast, bagel, muffin, roll, naan, roti, chapati, paratha, tortilla, pita

### Fruits
- **Cup(s)**: berry, berries, grape
- **Piece(s)**: apple, banana, orange, fruit, melon, mango, pineapple

### Vegetables
- **Plate**: salad
- **Piece(s)**: potato, aloo (Indian potato)
- **Cup(s)**: vegetable, broccoli, carrot, spinach, kale, lettuce, tomato, corn, pea, bean, okra, bhindi, gobi, palak

### Meats
- **Skewer(s)**: kebab
- **oz**: chicken, beef, steak, fish, meat, pork, lamb, turkey, salmon, tuna, shrimp, prawn, tandoori, tikka

### Eggs
- **Piece(s)**: egg, omelet, omelette

### Indian Dishes
- **Piece(s)**: samosa, pakora
- **Bowl**: curry, masala, dal, paneer, chaat, korma, vindaloo, saag, chana

### Italian Dishes
- **Slice(s)**: pizza
- **Serving(s)**: lasagna, pasta, risotto, gnocchi

### Mexican Dishes
- **Piece(s)**: taco
- **Serving(s)**: burrito, enchilada, quesadilla, nachos

### Asian Dishes
- **Piece(s)**: sushi, roll, dumpling, spring roll
- **Serving(s)**: stir fry, fried rice, pad thai

### Snacks
- **Serving(s)**: chip, crisp, snack, nut, candy, chocolate, cookie, cracker

## Default Unit

If the app doesn't recognize the food item you've entered, it will default to "serving(s)" as the unit.

## Customizing Units

Even though the app suggests units automatically, you can always change the unit to whatever you prefer by selecting a different option from the dropdown menu.

## Predefined Serving Sizes

For some common foods, the app also provides predefined serving size options with appropriate calorie adjustments:

### Fruits
- Apple, banana, orange: small, medium, large

### Proteins
- Chicken breast: 3 oz, 4 oz, 6 oz, 8 oz
- Steak: 4 oz, 6 oz, 8 oz, 12 oz
- Salmon: 3 oz, 4 oz, 6 oz, 8 oz
- Egg: 1 egg, 2 eggs, 3 eggs

### Grains
- Rice, pasta, cereal: 1/2 cup, 1 cup, 1.5 cups, 2 cups
- Bread: 1 slice, 2 slices

### Vegetables
- Broccoli, carrots: 1/2 cup, 1 cup, 2 cups
- Salad: small, medium, large

### Dairy
- Milk: 1/2 cup, 1 cup, 2 cups
- Yogurt: small (4 oz), regular (8 oz), large (12 oz)
- Cheese: 1 slice, 2 slices, 1 oz, 2 oz

### Snacks
- Chips: small bag (1 oz), medium bag (2.5 oz), large bag (5 oz)
- Nuts: 1/4 cup, 1/2 cup, 1 cup
- Chocolate: small piece, standard bar, large bar
